## 1.2 Detailed Steps Explanation

### **STEP 1: Preparation (5 minutes)**
- Energy manager arrives at work
- Starts computer
- Gathers information (historical prices, current thresholds)

### **STEP 2: Website Access (5 minutes)**
- Opens web browser
- Types energy price website URL
- Website loads slowly
- Logs in with username and password
- Sometimes password doesn't work → Has to reset

### **STEP 3: Find Price (10 minutes)**
- Looks through multiple pages on website
- Finds "Current Market Price" section
- Reads the number: $0.087/kWh
- May check multiple times because unsure
- Writes number down on paper

### **STEP 4: Analyze (10 minutes)**
- Thinks: "Is this price good or bad?"
- Compares to:
  - Yesterday's price (was $0.092)
  - Last week's average (was $0.089)
  - Predefined thresholds
- Manually calculates if it's an opportunity
- May call colleague to confirm interpretation

### **STEP 5: Decide (5 minutes)**
- Makes decision based on analysis:
  - "Is it BUY time?" (price very low)
  - "Is it HIGH time?" (price very high)
  - "Is it NORMAL?" (price acceptable)
- Writes decision on paper or sticky note

### **STEP 6: Alert Team (10 minutes)**
- Decides who needs to know
- Composes an email from scratch
- Writes subject and message
- Finds correct email addresses (may search)
- Clicks send
- Waits to confirm sent

### **STEP 7: Record Data (10 minutes)**
- Opens Excel spreadsheet
- Finds correct worksheet (by date)
- Finds correct row (by time)
- Types in data manually:
  - Timestamp
  - Price
  - Decision
  - Who was alerted
  - Any notes
- Saves file
- Worries about losing file if computer crashes

## 1.6 Current Problems with This Process

### **Problem 1: Very Time-Consuming** ⏰

| Current | Ideal |
|---------|-------|
| 55 min per check | <1 second per check |
| 687.5 hours/year | Almost 0 hours/year |
| 100% manual effort | 100% automated |

**Impact:** Manager could do other important work instead

---

### **Problem 2: Misses Opportunities Outside Work Hours** 🌙

**Today:**
```
Prices checked at:
├─ 8:00 AM ✓
├─ 12:00 PM ✓
├─ 4:00 PM ✓
└─ 2:00 AM ✗ (Nobody checking!)
└─ 11:00 PM ✗ (Nobody checking!)
```

**Reality:** Energy prices change 24/7, but only checked during work hours

**Result:** ~40% of low-price opportunities missed

**Example:**
- Price drops to $0.075/kWh at 2 AM
- Nobody sees it until 8 AM
- By 8 AM, price already went back up to $0.095/kWh
- **Lost opportunity to buy cheap!**

---

### **Problem 3: Delayed Alerts** ⏳

**Current Process:**
```
Price changes at: 2:30 PM
Energy manager checks at: 4:00 PM (90 min delay!)
Email sent to team at: 4:15 PM (105 min delay!)
Team can respond at: 4:30 PM+ (still delayed)
```

**If price is HIGH:** 
- Should reduce consumption IMMEDIATELY
- But delayed alert means already using expensive power
- By the time team responds, the high-price window has passed

---

### **Problem 4: Manual Entry Errors** 📝

**Common mistakes:**
- Typos in spreadsheet: $0.087 typed as $0.877 (10x wrong!)
- Wrong date entered
- Forgot to add decimal point
- Duplicate entries
- Missing data

**Result:** Data quality is poor, hard to analyze trends

---

### **Problem 5: Inconsistent Decision Making** 🤔

**Same price, different decisions:**

```
Monday: Price $0.085
Manager: "This is LOW, send BUY alert"

Wednesday: Price $0.085
Manager: "This is normal, no alert needed"

Problem: Same price, different decision!
Why? Manager tired, distracted, inconsistent threshold application
```

---

### **Problem 6: Hard to See Trends** 📊

**Current system (Excel spreadsheet):**
```
Date     | Price
May 1    | 0.087
May 2    | 0.089
May 3    | 0.091
May 4    | 0.085
May 5    | 0.093
May 6    | 0.088
May 7    | 0.090
May 8    | 0.092
...
```

**Questions that are hard to answer:**
- "What's the average price this month?" (Have to calculate manually)
- "What day has lowest price?" (Have to scan all rows)
- "Is price trending up or down?" (Have to visualize manually)
- "Can we forecast next week's price?" (Not possible with manual data)

---

### **Problem 7: Not Scalable** 📈

**Current system:**
```
1 location = 1 manager checking 3x/day
5 locations = 5 managers checking 3x/day each = 15 hours/day!
10 locations = 10 managers checking 3x/day each = 30 hours/day!
```

**Reality:** Company can't grow without hiring more people just to check prices

## 1.7 Business Context

### **Why Energy Prices Matter**

For a typical office building:
- **Monthly energy cost:** $5,000-$10,000
- **Yearly energy cost:** $60,000-$120,000

Energy is often the **2nd or 3rd largest operating expense** (after salaries and rent)

**Even 1% savings = $600-$1,200/year**

**With this process, we can save 3-5% = $1,800-$6,000/year**

---

### **Decision Points**

Energy manager needs to answer:

**Question 1: "Should we buy energy now?"**
- Answer: If price is LOW (< $0.08/kWh) → YES
- Action: Alert procurement team to buy

**Question 2: "Should we reduce consumption now?"**
- Answer: If price is HIGH (> $0.12/kWh) → YES
- Action: Alert operations team to reduce usage

**Question 3: "Should we do anything?"**
- Answer: If price is NORMAL ($0.08-0.12/kWh) → NO
- Action: Continue normal operations
